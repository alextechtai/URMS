Sustainability at URMS

Responsibility
Meets Reality

As a global raw material distributor, URMS occupies a critical point of leverage in the supply chain, one we use deliberately.

Our sourcing decisions are anchored to producers whose investments in decarbonization, circular chemistry, and energy transition represent structural shifts in how materials are made, not merely regulatory compliance. Clients access verified, high-performance materials without the complexity of managing those relationships independently.

Our Framework

Certified, Traceable,
Accountable

01 / Certification

URMS holds Global Recycled Standard (GRS) certification, providing full chain-of-custody traceability on recycled content across our portfolio, a standard now required across regulatory environments in Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific. This positions our clients to meet tightening documentation requirements with confidence.

02 / Material Discipline

Our material selection is guided by where polymers create measurable real-world impact. Food-grade applications demand high-purity grades that actively reduce spoilage and extend shelf life. Medical applications require a level of sourcing discipline that goes beyond commercial considerations. We source specialized grades compliant with ISO 10993, ISO 13485, and related biocompatibility and device manufacturing standards, because in clinical environments, material specification is a matter of patient safety, not procurement preference. That same discipline extends across every sector we serve, from agriculture and construction to industrial applications, where we are equally committed to supplying materials that meet the environmental and performance certifications each end market requires. Compliance confidence, regardless of application, is what we consider a baseline obligation.

03 / Measurement

URMS has integrated carbon footprint and energy intensity tracking into its sourcing decision framework, keeping our operations aligned with the evolving landscape of international sustainability regulations and reporting standards. As supply chain transparency requirements tighten globally, we are positioned to provide clients with the verified data and certified materials their own compliance obligations demand.

At URMS, sustainability is not the conclusion of our supply chain story. It is the standard against which every decision within it is made.
